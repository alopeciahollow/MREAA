Leadership

Nominations

Nominate yourself or a fellow member for the National Operating Board. Every seat is won through a merit-based, deliverable-driven election.

Open Positions

The National Operating Board

Seven officers elected by the voting membership to two-year terms, with a limit of two consecutive terms in the same position.

Operating President

Leads the National Operating Board and the Association's day-to-day affairs. Filled by the sitting President-Select after a 90-day transition.

Operating Vice President

Elected as President-Select and automatically becomes Operating President when the current term ends.

Operating Treasurer

Manages dues, operating budgets, and quarterly financial reports, with dual authorization on all payments.

Operating Secretary

Keeps minutes of all board and member meetings and distributes them within seven days.

Chapter Leads

Three seats, for Boston, New York, and Los Angeles. Each runs quarterly chapter dinners and sits on the national board.

Who Can Run

Any member in good standing, including non-alumni members. Nominees must accept and submit a Deliverable Plan to appear on the ballot.

How It Works

The Election Process

1. Nominations · 3 weeks

Candidates submit a Statement of Intent and a Deliverable Plan with at least three SMART goals: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.

2. Presentation · 1 week

Candidate platforms go out to the full membership, and a virtual Town Hall may be held for questions.

3. Digital Voting · 1 week

Secure online voting, one vote per verified voting member in good standing.

4. Transition

New officers are seated at the start of the next fiscal quarter, beginning a 90-day handover with outgoing officers.
