Nominate yourself or a fellow member for the Operating Group or a Regional Lead role. Nominations are open Oct. 12 – Nov. 9.

Open Positions

Operating Group Election

Four officers elected by the voting membership to lead the Association for two-year terms.

President

Leads the Operating Group and the Association's day-to-day affairs.

Vice President

Elected as President-Select and becomes President when the current term ends.

Treasurer

Manages dues, operating budgets, and quarterly financial reports.

Secretary

Keeps minutes of board and member meetings and shares them within seven days.

Open Positions

Regional Leads

Regional Leads bring members together in their area, hosting quarterly dinners and local programming.

Los Angeles

Members across Southern California.

San Francisco

Members across the Bay Area.

New York

Members across the New York metro area.

Florida

Members across the state of Florida.

Texas

Members across the state of Texas.

Any member in good standing may run, including non-alumni members.
